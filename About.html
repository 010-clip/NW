Why we built NetWeave

The next great idea shouldn't be gated behind a R40,000 build.

NW

The NetWeave team

Cape Town · Est. 2026

We started NetWeave because we kept meeting the same person: a designer, developer, founder or freelancer with a real idea and no way to put it online without spending a fortune or surrendering their brand to a platform.

The big site builders were built for scale, not for craft. They demand monthly fees before you've published a single page, lock you into templates that look like everyone else's, and quietly turn your visitors into their visitors. Agency builds and custom stacks solve that — but they start at five figures and take months.

So we built the thing we wished existed. NetWeave gives creators, freelancers, agencies and small businesses a genuine site of their own — the same edge infrastructure a major platform runs on — for R0 to launch. You pay as you grow, and only for the tools you actually turn on.

We're based in Cape Town and we build for two audiences at once: creators and teams in emerging markets who've been underserved by platforms priced in dollars, and anyone anywhere who refuses to look like everyone else.

If you're building something right now — we built this for you.
