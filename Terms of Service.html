01

Zero-Cost Launch & Pay-As-You-Grow Model

NetWeave is free to launch. There is no trial, no demo paywall, and no credit card required to build and publish your first site. Creating an account, designing pages, and going live on a yoursite.netweave.io subdomain costs R0 — indefinitely.

How you pay

As your project grows, you can turn on optional add-ons — such as a custom domain, advanced analytics, additional storage, form handling, or priority infrastructure. Each add-on is priced individually and monthly. You pay only for what you enable, and you can disable any add-on at any time from your dashboard.

What we do not charge

  • No platform fee on transactions you process through your site.
  • No revenue share on your work, products, or content.
  • No per-page, per-component, or per-publish charges.
  • No "gotcha" overage fees — if an add-on would exceed its limit, we notify you before billing changes.

Changes to pricing

We may introduce new add-ons or adjust the price of existing ones. If we change the price of an add-on you already have, we'll give you at least 30 days' notice by email. You can cancel the add-on before the change takes effect and keep using your site on the free plan.

Billing & refunds

Add-ons are billed monthly in advance. If you cancel mid-cycle, the add-on remains active until the end of the paid period. We don't offer partial-month refunds, except where required by law or where we've made a billing error. If we do charge you in error, tell us within 60 days and we'll refund the full amount.

02

Site Ownership & Data Control

You own your site. Everything you create on NetWeave — your pages, your design, your images, your text, your domain (once purchased), and your customer data — belongs to you. NetWeave claims no ownership, no license beyond what's needed to run the platform, and no right to monetise your content.

What we need from you

To operate the platform, we need a limited licence to host, cache, and serve your content to visitors — and to display it back to you inside the editor. This licence ends the moment you delete the content or close your account. It does not give us the right to reuse your content for marketing, resell it, or share it with third parties beyond the infrastructure providers required to run NetWeave.

Your data, your export

At any time, you can export your site's content, media and form submissions from your dashboard in standard formats (JSON, CSV, and original media files). You can also delete individual pages, media assets, or your entire account. When you delete your account, we permanently erase your content within 30 days, except where we're required by law to retain specific records.

Your visitors' data

Form submissions, analytics events and any other data your site collects from its visitors belong to you. We process that data on your behalf as a data processor, under our Data Processing Addendum. You are the data controller and are responsible for making sure your site complies with applicable privacy laws — including displaying an appropriate privacy policy and obtaining consent where required.

Domains

If you connect a custom domain to your NetWeave site, you remain the registered owner of that domain. If the domain is registered through NetWeave, you can transfer it out at any time. We never lock a domain to the platform as a condition of using it.

03

Acceptable Use Policy

NetWeave is a general-purpose website builder. You can use it to build almost anything — a portfolio, a landing page, a blog, a store, a community, a documentation site, a game, or something we haven't imagined yet. The limit isn't what you build; it's how you use it.

You may not use NetWeave to

  • Publish content that is illegal in your jurisdiction or ours, including content that infringes copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property rights.
  • Distribute malware, phishing pages, spam, or deceptive look-alike sites designed to defraud visitors.
  • Harass, threaten, defame, or target individuals or groups with hateful or violent content.
  • Sell or promote goods or services that are themselves illegal — including unlicensed pharmaceuticals, weapons, exploitative material, or financial fraud.
  • Circumvent platform limits, resell NetWeave infrastructure without authorisation, or run bots that abuse the hosting, CDN, or form systems.
  • Process payment card data directly on NetWeave pages without using an approved payment processor.
  • Impersonate another person, brand, or organisation in a way that misleads visitors about your identity or affiliation.

Commercial use is fine

You can use NetWeave for commercial purposes — client work, freelance projects, product sites, digital storefronts, and so on. There's no restriction on earning money through your site.

Enforcement

If we believe your site violates this policy, we'll contact you with specifics and give you a reasonable window to fix the issue. In serious cases — illegal content, active fraud, or a threat to platform security — we may suspend the site immediately while we investigate. If we suspend a site in error, we'll restore it and refund any add-ons affected.

You can appeal any enforcement action by emailing legal@netweave.io. We review every appeal within five working days.

04

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms as the platform evolves. When we make material changes — anything that affects your rights, your data, or how much you pay — we'll notify you by email at least 30 days before the change takes effect, and we'll highlight the changes clearly.

If you don't agree with an update, you can stop using NetWeave and export your content before the change takes effect. Continuing to use the platform after the effective date means you accept the updated terms.

Minor changes — typo corrections, clarifications, updated contact details — take effect immediately. We'll always keep a version history of these terms publicly accessible.

Contact

If you have questions about these terms, your data, or anything else — email us before you sign up. We answer plainly.
